## Titus 1:1 (id: 10056001001)

## Titus 1:2 (id: 10056001002)

## Titus 1:3 (id: 10056001003)

## Titus 1:4 (id: 10056001004)

4Zuwa ga Titus, ɗana na gaske a tarayyarmu cikin bangaskiya. Alheri da salama daga Allah Uba da kuma Kiristi Yesu Mai Cetonmu, su kasance tare da kai.


## Titus 1:5 (id: 10056001005)

5Dalilin da ya sa na bar ka a Kirit shi ne don ka daidaita abubuwan da suka rage da ba a kammala ba, ka kuma naɗa dattawa a kowane gari, yadda na umarce ka.


## Titus 1:6 (id: 10056001006)

6Dole dattijo yă kasance marar aibi, mijin mace guda, mutumin da ’ya’yansa masu bi ne, waɗanda kuma ba a san su da lalata ko rashin biyayya ba.


## Titus 1:7 (id: 10056001007)

7Da yake an danƙa wa mai kula da ikkilisiya riƙon amana saboda Allah, dole yă zama marar aibi ba mai girman kai ba, ba mai saurin fushi ba, ba mai buguwa ba, ba mai rikici ba, ba kuma mai son ƙazamar riba ba.


## Titus 1:8 (id: 10056001008)

8A maimakon haka dole yă zama mai karɓan baƙi, mai son abu nagari, mai kamunkai, mai adalci, mai tsarki da kuma natsattse.


## Titus 1:9 (id: 10056001009)

9Dole yă riƙe tabbataccen saƙon nan kam\-kam yadda aka koyar, don yă iya ƙarfafa waɗansu da sahihiyar koyarwa yă kuma ƙaryata waɗanda suke gāba da shi.


## Titus 1:10 (id: 10056001010)

10Gama akwai ’yan tawaye da yawa, masu surutun banza da masu ruɗi, musamman ƙungiyar masu kaciya.


## Titus 1:11 (id: 10056001011)

11Dole a hana su yin magana, domin suna kawo rushewar iyalai ta wurin koyar da abubuwan da bai kamata su koyar ba don neman ƙazamar riba.


## Titus 1:12 (id: 10056001012)

12Wani daga cikin annabawansu ma ya ce, “Kiretawa a kullum maƙaryata ne, mugayen dabbobi, ragwaye haɗamammu.”


## Titus 1:13 (id: 10056001013)

## Titus 1:14 (id: 10056001014)

## Titus 1:15 (id: 10056001015)

## Titus 1:16 (id: 10056001016)

## Titus 2:9 (id: 10056002009)
