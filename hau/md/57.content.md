## Filemon 1:1 (id: 10057001001)

1Bulus, ɗan kurkuku saboda Kiristi Yesu, da kuma Timoti ɗan’uwanmu, Zuwa ga Filemon ƙaunataccen abokinmu da kuma abokin aikinmu,


## Filemon 1:2 (id: 10057001002)

2zuwa kuma ga Affiya ’yar’uwarmu, zuwa kuma ga Arkiffus abokin famarmu, da zuwa kuma ga ikkilisiyar da take taruwa a gidanka.


## Filemon 1:3 (id: 10057001003)

3Alheri da salama daga Allah Ubanmu da kuma Ubangiji Yesu Kiristi, su kasance tare da ku.


## Filemon 1:4 (id: 10057001004)

4Kullum nakan gode wa Allahna sa’ad da nake tuna da kai cikin addu’o’ina,


## Filemon 1:5 (id: 10057001005)

5domin ina jin labari bangaskiyarka cikin Ubangiji Yesu da kuma ƙaunarka da kake yi saboda dukan tsarkaka.


## Filemon 1:6 (id: 10057001006)

6Ina addu’a ka zama mai ƙwazo cikin shaida bangaskiyarka, domin ka sami cikakken fahimta ta kowane abin da yake nagarin da muke da shi cikin Kiristi.


## Filemon 1:7 (id: 10057001007)

7Ƙaunarka ta sa ni farin ciki ƙwarai ta kuma ƙarfafa ni, domin kai, ɗan’uwa, ka wartsake zukatan tsarkaka.


## Filemon 1:8 (id: 10057001008)

8Saboda haka, ko da yake cikin Kiristi ina iya yin ƙarfin hali in umarce ka ka yi abin da ya kamata,


## Filemon 1:9 (id: 10057001009)

9duk da haka ina roƙonka saboda ƙauna, na fi so in roƙe ka, ni Bulus tsoho, a yanzu kuma ga ni ɗan sarƙa saboda Kiristi Yesu.


## Filemon 1:10 (id: 10057001010)

10Ina roƙonka saboda ɗana Onesimus, wanda ya zama ɗana yayinda nake cikin sarƙoƙi.


## Filemon 1:11 (id: 10057001011)

11Dā kam, ba shi da amfani a gare ka, amma yanzu ya zama da amfani a gare ka da kuma gare ni.


## Filemon 1:12 (id: 10057001012)

12Ina aike shi yă koma gare ka, shi da yake zuciyata.


## Filemon 1:13 (id: 10057001013)

13Na so in riƙe shi a wurina a madadinka domin yă taimake ni yayinda nake cikin sarƙoƙi saboda bishara.


## Filemon 1:14 (id: 10057001014)

14Sai dai ban so in yi kome ban da yardarka ba, domin kowane alherin da za ka yi, yă fito daga zuciyarka, ba na tilas ba.


## Filemon 1:15 (id: 10057001015)

15Wataƙila abin da ya sa aka raba ka da shi na ɗan lokaci ƙanƙani shi ne don ka same shi da amfani


## Filemon 1:16 (id: 10057001016)

16ba kamar bawa kuma ba, sai dai fiye da bawa, a matsayin ƙaunataccen ɗan’uwa. Shi ƙaunatacce ne sosai a gare ni amma a gare ka ya ma fi zama ƙaunatacce, kamar mutum da kuma kamar ɗan’uwa a cikin Ubangiji.


## Filemon 1:17 (id: 10057001017)

17Saboda haka in ka ɗauke ni a kan ni abokin tarayya ne, sai ka marabce shi kamar yadda za ka marabce ni.


## Filemon 1:18 (id: 10057001018)

18In ya yi maka wani laifi ko kuma yana riƙe maka wani bashi, sai ka mai da shi a kaina.


## Filemon 1:19 (id: 10057001019)

19Ni, Bulus, nake rubuta wannan da hannuna. Zan biya ka, kada ma a yi zancen ranka da kake riƙe mini bashi.


## Filemon 1:20 (id: 10057001020)

20Ina fata, ɗan’uwa, zan sami amfani daga gare ka cikin Ubangiji; ka wartsake zuciyata cikin Kiristi.


## Filemon 1:21 (id: 10057001021)

21Da tabbacin biyayyarka, ina rubuta maka, da sanin za ka yi fiye da abin da na ce.


## Filemon 1:22 (id: 10057001022)

22Abu guda ya rage, ka shirya mini masauƙi, domin ina bege za a mayar da ni gare ku albarkacin addu’o’inku.


## Filemon 1:23 (id: 10057001023)

23Efafaras, abokin tarayyata a kurkuku cikin Kiristi Yesu, yana gaishe ka.


## Filemon 1:24 (id: 10057001024)

24Haka kuma Markus, Aristarkus, Demas da kuma Luka, abokan aikina.


## Filemon 1:25 (id: 10057001025)

25Alherin Ubangiji Yesu Kiristi yă kasance da ruhunku.
